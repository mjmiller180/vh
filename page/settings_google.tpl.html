Google Sheet for orders

VineSmyths can add your orders to a Google Sheet (the DASHBOARD tab of your "Reviews" workbook): date, order number, picture, status ORDERED, title, ETV. Rows are added in the first empty row with the same formatting and dropdowns as the row above, only the columns it knows are written, and an order number already on the sheet is never added twice. Try it on a copy of your sheet first. 


	


	

		Pictures from Google Drive (for reviews)
		

			Adds an "Add from Google Drive…" button to Amazon's review form. It shows your newest pictures (or the folder
			below) with small previews; the ones you tick are attached to the review. VineSmyths only reads Drive,
			and only when you open that window. It needs the extra "view your Drive files" permission: after switching
			this on, press Sign in to Google above again so Google can ask you.
		

		

			
		

		

			
			
		

	


	

		Review form tools
		

			On Amazon's "write a review" page: a formatting toolbar, saved phrases and templates, an automatic draft of your
			title and text, and pasting or dropping pictures into Amazon's upload box. When you press Submit, the star
			rating, today's date and the status also go to your sheet (if the sheet link above is switched on).
