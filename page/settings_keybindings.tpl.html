This activates the keyboard shortcuts.

Press the start key on a Vine page and VineSmyths pages through the queue on a timer, stopping at the first page with an item you have not pinned (items already hidden are ignored). Space pauses or resumes, Escape stops and shows the totals. Pinning or hiding the items and pressing Space continues. Pages are never turned faster than every 2 seconds. Click the status box to enlarge it.

Patrol (patrol key) loops over a range of pages again and again and stops when something new shows up: use it when most items are already hidden. Jump keys start a normal session at a page you choose.
